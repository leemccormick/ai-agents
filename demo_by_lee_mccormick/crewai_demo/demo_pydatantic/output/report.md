## 🤖 Agentic AI Definition

Agentic AI is defined as an autonomous AI system capable of planning, reasoning, and acting to complete tasks with minimal oversight. This core definition provides the foundational understanding of this cutting-edge technology. The capacity for autonomous operation distinguishes Agentic AI from other forms of AI. The systems can independently set goals, devise strategies, and execute actions to achieve those objectives. The need for minimal oversight enhances efficiency and autonomy. This means that Agentic AI can perform complex tasks without extensive human intervention, thereby revolutionizing numerous sectors. The characteristics of planning, reasoning, and acting are essential for creating systems that can respond to dynamic situations, make informed decisions, and achieve desired outcomes. This capability underscores the transformative potential of agentic AI (What is agentic AI? (Definition and 2025 guide), 2025).
